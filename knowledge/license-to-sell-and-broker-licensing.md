## Check the License to Sell before reserving
step: reservation
see-also: Who may sell real estate

Under PD 957 a developer must register a subdivision or condominium project and hold a License to Sell from the housing department (DHSUD, formerly HLURB) before selling or advertising its lots or units, pre-selling included. Before taking a reservation on a project, confirm its License to Sell number, which developers show on their marketing.

## Who may sell real estate
step: compliance

Under RA 9646 a real estate broker must be licensed by the Professional Regulation Commission after passing the board exam. A real estate salesperson must be accredited and must work under the supervision of a licensed broker, not on their own; their commission is paid through that broker. Licensed brokers keep their license current with continuing professional development.
