## Lead stages at Heir Estates
step: pipeline
next: Reserved stage and the papers clock

A lead moves through New, Contacted, Viewing and Reserved (the four open stages), and ends Closed or Lost. A lost lead always has a reason written down. Hot, Warm and Cold are a separate tag for how keen the client is, and don't change the stage. An open lead with no note, WhatsApp message or stage change for over 7 days counts as gone quiet.

## Reserved stage and the papers clock
step: reservation
requires: Buyer's papers checklist
next: The 30-day papers deadline
see-also: Check the License to Sell before reserving

Moving a lead to Reserved means the buyer has paid the reservation and starts the papers clock. Moving the lead back to New, Contacted or Viewing stops the clock. Reserved buyers show on the Dashboard under Papers due until all their papers are verified.

## Buyer's papers checklist
step: papers
see-also: Contract to Sell

Five folders on the lead's Files tab: IDs & TIN, Proof of income, Reservation, Contract to Sell, and Payments & PDCs. Each is Missing, Uploaded (not yet checked), Verified or Rejected. A rejection carries the reason the buyer must fix, for example a blurry ID or the wrong month's payslip.

## The 30-day papers deadline
step: papers

From the day a lead is Reserved, the buyer has 30 days to complete four of the papers: IDs & TIN, Proof of income, Reservation, and Payments & PDCs. The Contract to Sell isn't timed, because it comes later from the developer. The Dashboard counts the days left and turns red in the last week.

## Contract to Sell
step: cts
see-also: Maceda Law: buyers with two or more years paid

The Contract to Sell comes from the developer after the reservation and the buyer's papers. It sets the payment schedule; title passes to the buyer only once the price is fully paid. File the signed copy in the lead's Contract to Sell folder.
