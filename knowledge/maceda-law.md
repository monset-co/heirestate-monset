## Maceda Law: who it covers
step: cts
next: Maceda Law: buyers with two or more years paid
next: Maceda Law: buyers with less than two years paid

RA 6552 protects people buying residential real estate on installment, condominium units included. It doesn't cover industrial lots, commercial buildings, or sales to tenants under the agrarian reform law. Down payments, deposits and options on the contract count as payments made.

Under it the buyer may also sell or assign their rights to someone else before the contract is cancelled, and may pay the full balance at any time without interest.

## Maceda Law: buyers with two or more years paid
step: cts

A buyer who has paid at least two years of installments and then misses payments gets a grace period of one month for every year of installments paid, without interest. They can use this once in every five years of the contract.

If the contract is then cancelled, the seller must refund the cash surrender value: 50% of everything paid, plus 5% more for each year beyond five years of installments, up to 90%. The cancellation takes effect only 30 days after the buyer receives a notice of cancellation by notarial act, and after the refund is paid in full.

## Maceda Law: buyers with less than two years paid
step: cts

A buyer who has paid less than two years of installments gets a grace period of at least 60 days from the date the installment was due. If they still don't pay, the seller may cancel the contract 30 days after the buyer receives a notice of cancellation or demand by notarial act. There is no required refund at this level.
